Smart locks are one of the most tempting smart home upgrades, but UK doors make the choice awkward. Euro cylinders, multipoint locks, thumb turns, rental agreements and insurance wording all matter. Do not buy purely because a lock works with Alexa.

For a first smart lock, the safest route is usually a **retrofit lock** that fits on the inside of the door. That lets you keep a normal key outside while adding phone, keypad or automation control inside.

## Best First Smart Lock: SwitchBot Smart Lock

The **SwitchBot Smart Lock** is the smart lock I would check first if you are nervous about changing your front door. It is a retrofit device, so it is less intimidating than replacing the whole lock mechanism.

- You rent or may move.
- You want to try smart locking without a full door hardware change.

## UK Door Compatibility Checklist

Before buying any smart lock, check:

- Does your door use a euro cylinder, night latch or another lock type?
- Can the key be left inside while another key works outside?
- Does the handle need lifting before the door can lock?
- Is your current cylinder secure enough, or should it be upgraded first?
- Will the lock interfere with your home insurance wording?
- Can you still unlock manually if the battery dies?

## Smart Lock Security Basics

Do not make the smart lock the only security upgrade. It should sit alongside proper door hardware, sensible account security and entry-point alerts.

Use:

- Strong app passwords.
- Two-factor authentication where available.
- A physical key backup.
- App access reviews when someone moves out.
- A doorbell or camera covering the front door.
