Outdoor CCTV cameras are where smart home security becomes genuinely useful: front paths, side gates, sheds, garages, driveways and back gardens are all easier to monitor with the right camera. The hard part is choosing between wired, battery and solar options without getting trapped by subscription costs.

If you also want cameras for pets, halls, living rooms or elderly-relative check-ins, pair this with our [best indoor CCTV cameras UK guide](/blog/best-indoor-cctv-cameras-uk/). Outdoor cameras need weatherproofing and deterrence; indoor cameras need privacy controls.

For most UK homes, the practical split is simple. Choose **Ring or Blink** if you already live in the Alexa/Amazon ecosystem and want easy alerts. Choose **Tapo** if you want stronger local-storage value. Choose **eufy** if you want a more complete multi-camera security kit with a home base.

## Wired vs Battery vs Solar

Choose **wired outdoor CCTV** if:

- The camera location is busy.
- You can route power safely.
- You do not want charging routines.
- You want more reliable continuous operation.

Choose **battery outdoor CCTV** if:

- You rent or cannot easily drill.
- You want fast installation.
- The camera only sees occasional motion.
- You can reach the camera for charging.

Choose **solar-assisted outdoor CCTV** if:

- The camera gets enough daylight.
- You accept that UK winter and shade may reduce charging.
- You still want a backup charging plan.

## UK Privacy and Placement

Outdoor CCTV is allowed for home security, but do not be careless with the camera angle. Try to record your own property, not neighbours' windows, gardens or shared private areas. Use privacy zones where available and consider visible signage if your camera captures visitors, delivery drivers or public areas.

Good outdoor placements include:

- Front path and porch.
- Back door.
- Side gate.
- Garage or driveway.
- Shed entrance.

Weak placements include:

- Directly at a neighbour's window.
- Across a shared garden.
- Too high to identify faces.
- Too low where it can be grabbed.
- Too far from Wi-Fi.
